Job Sources & Attribution

WintWorks organizes job listings supplied by third-party job platforms. Every listing identifies its source and links directly to the source listing. WintWorks does not accept applications or claim ownership of third-party job content.

Our sources

  • The Muse — listings link back to their original The Muse pages.
  • Jobicy — each listing retains its original Jobicy URL.
  • Remote OK — Remote OK is named on each listing and linked with a standard followed link.
  • Remotive — Remotive is named as the source and each listing links back to Remotive.
  • Arbeitnow — each listing links back to its Arbeitnow source page.
  • Adzuna — Adzuna listings display the required “Jobs by Adzuna” credit and link back to Adzuna.

Accuracy and ownership

Job details can change or expire at any time. Verify the role, employer, location, salary and application requirements on the linked source page before applying. Job descriptions, employer names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.

WintWorks is an independent aggregation service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the listed employers or job platforms unless expressly stated.

Corrections and removal

If you represent an employer or source and need a listing corrected or removed, please contact us with the listing URL.
