AL-BAYAN · LIVE DEMO

Publish your free live demo — in 2 minutes

No GitHub. No account required. No credit card. Drag & drop, get an HTTPS link, paste it into the Codester Demo URL field.

1

Extract the ZIP

Extract netlify-drop.zip. You will get a folder named al-bayan containing index.html. This is the whole website — it's already ready.

2

Open Netlify Drop

In your browser, open https://app.netlify.com/drop

3

Drag the folder onto the page

Drag the al-bayan folder (not the ZIP) from your computer and drop it on the Netlify Drop area. Uploading takes about one minute.

4

Get your live HTTPS link

Netlify instantly gives you a link like https://something-special.netlify.app — HTTPS, fast, worldwide.

5

Paste it into Codester

Copy that link and paste it into the Demo URL field in your Codester submission form. Done.

Optional (recommended): click Site configuration → Change site name on Netlify to set a friendly name, e.g. albayan-demo → https://albayan-demo.netlify.app. The link never changes unless you rename the site.
Note: Netlify's site name is public. Anyone who has the link can view the demo. The demo site contains no personal data — it's the template's demo content only. The php/contact.php form will work as a demo (no email sending) on Netlify; email sending requires PHP hosting or a form service (documented in the package).
Taking it down later: on Netlify, open the site → Site configuration → Danger zone → Delete site. Takes one click.
