About WintWorks

WintWorks.com is an automated job board for the United States, major European markets and worldwide remote roles. It collects job openings from trusted public job platforms and publishes them in one clean, searchable place.

How it works

  • Automatic collection. Our system connects to approved job data sources, including The Muse, Jobicy, Remote OK, Remotive, Arbeitnow and Adzuna, several times a day.
  • Smart region & country detection. Every listing is auto-tagged 🇺🇸 USA, 🇪🇺 Europe or 🌍 Worldwide, and each job is matched to its country (UK, Germany, France, Netherlands, Poland, Spain, Sweden and 40+ more) — filter by region, by country, or both.
  • Deduplication & formatting. Duplicate postings from multiple sources are merged, and every job is formatted with location, type, salary and category.
  • Always up to date. New jobs appear automatically — you never pay, sign up, or search anywhere else.

Why is it free?

WintWorks is supported by advertising and optional sponsored job placements. Job seekers always browse completely free. Employers can promote their openings on the Advertise page.

Our data sources

  • The Muse — hundreds of thousands of US company listings
  • Jobicy — remote jobs with salary data
  • Remote OK — remote tech roles
  • Remotive — remote roles for US and Europe-based applicants
  • Arbeitnow — European and UK job listings
  • Adzuna — local-market listings supplied through the Adzuna API and displayed under its attribution requirements
