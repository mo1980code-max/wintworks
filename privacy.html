Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

WintWorks ("WintWorks", "we", "us" or "our") operates https://wintworks.com. This policy explains what information and browser storage may be used when you visit the site, why they are used, the choices available to you, and how advertising services may process data.

1. Information we process

You do not need to create an account or provide your name or email address to browse job listings. The site and its service providers may process the following limited information:

  • Privacy choices: your consent selections are stored so the site can remember and respect them.
  • Site preferences: settings you request, such as theme and saved jobs, may be stored in your browser.
  • Performance cache: a temporary copy of job-listing data may be retained in browser storage to make repeat visits faster.
  • Technical and security data: our hosting provider may process IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps and similar log data to deliver and secure the website, prevent abuse and diagnose errors.
  • Advertising and consent data: if advertising is enabled, Google and participating advertising partners may process consent signals, device and browser information, IP address, page interactions and advertising events as described below.

2. Cookies, local storage and consent

WintWorks uses necessary browser storage for security, consent records and features requested by the user. Optional analytics and advertising storage are denied by default unless the visitor provides consent where consent is required.

Visitors can consent, refuse consent or manage individual purposes through the privacy interface. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP), which communicates consent choices to Google and participating advertising partners.

Storage categories

  • Necessary: supports security, consent records and features explicitly requested by you. This category cannot be disabled through the consent interface.
  • Preferences: remembers optional display and performance choices.
  • Analytics: may help us understand aggregate site usage if an analytics service is enabled and the required permission has been provided.
  • Advertising: may support ad delivery, measurement, frequency control, security, fraud prevention and, where you consent, personalised advertising.

3. Google AdSense and advertising partners

WintWorks uses Google AdSense publisher code. If ads are served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure advertisements, limit repeated ads, detect invalid traffic, prevent fraud and—where the visitor has consented—personalise advertising.

Choosing not to consent to personalised advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertising. Google may show non-personalised or limited ads where permitted, subject to the visitor's choices and applicable law.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google My Ad Center

4. Changing or withdrawing your choices

You can change or withdraw your optional consent at any time. Use the Privacy and cookie settings control in the footer or the button below. When Google's certified CMP applies, this control opens Google's consent-revocation flow.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred lawfully before withdrawal. You can also clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings, although doing so may remove saved jobs, theme settings and your recorded privacy choice.

5. Legal bases and privacy rights

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, optional advertising personalisation and optional analytics rely on consent. Necessary storage and technical processing may be used to provide the service, fulfil a user request, maintain security or pursue legitimate interests such as preventing abuse, subject to applicable law.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Because most WintWorks preferences remain only in your browser, we may not be able to identify or retrieve them from our systems.

6. Retention

Browser preferences and the consent record remain on your device until they are replaced, withdrawn or cleared through your browser. Hosting, security and advertising providers retain data according to their own documented retention schedules and applicable legal requirements.

7. External links and job applications

Job listings and guides may link to third-party job platforms, employers, government websites and other external services. WintWorks is not responsible for their content or privacy practices. When you follow an external link, that service's privacy policy and terms apply.

WintWorks does not accept, collect or process job applications. Selecting an application link redirects you to the identified source or application page.

8. Job-listing data

Job listings are obtained from identified third-party platforms and APIs. Job descriptions, company names, logos and related listing content remain the property of their respective publishers, employers or rights holders. For details, see our Job Sources & Attribution page.

9. Children's privacy

WintWorks is a job-search service intended for a general adult and working-age audience. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, advertising services, legal requirements or data-processing practices. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page. Where legally required, we will request consent again after material changes.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact WintWorks through the contact page or email [email protected].
