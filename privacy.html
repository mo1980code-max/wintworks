Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

WintWorks ("we", "us") operates wintworks.com. This policy explains the information and browser storage used when you visit the site, the choices available to you, and how advertising may operate.

1. Information and local storage

You do not need an account and we do not ask for your name or email to browse jobs. The site may use:

  • Necessary storage — records your privacy choice and supports security or a feature you request.
  • Preference storage — remembers settings such as theme and saved jobs in your browser.
  • Performance cache — may temporarily retain a copy of job data to make repeat visits faster.
  • Hosting logs — our hosting provider may process IP address, browser, requested page and timestamps for delivery, reliability and security.

2. Cookies, consent and advertising

Optional analytics and advertising storage are denied by default. You can accept all optional categories, reject them, or choose categories separately. Your decision is saved in your browser so that the site can respect it.

  • Analytics: if enabled in the future and you consent, analytics may be used to understand aggregate site usage.
  • Advertising: WintWorks may display advertising, including Google AdSense, after it is configured. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and—where you consent—personalisation.
  • European users: when AdSense begins serving ads, WintWorks will also use a Google-certified consent management platform for visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, as required by Google's publisher policy.

You can change or withdraw your optional choices at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer or this button:

You may also manage Google advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center.

3. External links

Job listings link to external employer websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party sites. Please review their policies.

4. Job data

Job postings shown on WintWorks are aggregated from public sources and remain the property of their original publishers. We do not collect job applications — applications are submitted on the employer's own website.

5. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us via the contact page or email hello@wintworks.com.
